# Audio sources

All recorded audio in this folder is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
It may be used, modified, and distributed without attribution.

- `Weapons/Pistol.wav`, `Weapons/Magnum.wav`, and `Weapons/Shotgun.wav`
  - Source: **Gunshots** by kurt
  - Edited into individual shots for this game.
  - https://opengameart.org/content/gunshots
- `Weapons/Reload.wav`
  - Source: **Gun Reload Sound Effects** by BMacZero
  - https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects
- `Weapons/Flamethrower.wav`
  - Source: **Fireplace Sound loop** by PagDev
  - Edited into a short fire sample for this game.
  - https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop
- `Weapons/Explosion.wav`
  - Source: **Mechanical Explosion** by Spring Spring
  - https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-explosion
- `Zombies/zombies/*.wav`
  - Source: **Zombies Sound Pack** by artisticdude
  - https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
- `World/HorrorAmbience.ogg`
  - Source: **Lost in a bad place (horror ambience loop)** by congusbongus
  - https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop
